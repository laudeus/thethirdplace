# The Third Place  
**Rebuilding Canadian Community Through the Blueprint of Odd Fellowship**  
*The Three Links: Friendship · Love · Truth*  
by Harold Laudeus · Common Ground Press · 2026  

A practical field guide to curing Canada’s “social recession” by reclaiming historic halls and rebuilding embodied, cross‑class community.

---

## 📘 About the book

We are facing a crisis of isolation. Across Canada, public life has retreated behind closed doors and paywalls. Trapped between rising commercial rents and the isolating glow of digital feeds, we are forced to pay an admission surcharge just to sit in public. When every room demands a credit-card swipe, belonging becomes transactional.

In *The Third Place*, researcher Harold Laudeus uncovers the forgotten blueprint of the Independent Order of Odd Fellows—a mutualist network that once housed millions of working people in self-funded, secular, intergenerational halls.

From the brick halls of rural Ontario to the industrial settlements of the Pacific coast, this practical field guide shows how modern citizens can reclaim historic spaces, establish radical cross-class rooms, and construct durable civic sanctuaries today.

This repository hosts the **source manuscript** (Markdown chapters, images, and appendices) for the upcoming paid ebook and print editions. It is intended primarily as:

- A project home for the book’s development and versioning  
- A public portfolio piece demonstrating research, writing, and civic strategy  
- A future hub for reader resources (e.g., the 90‑day blueprint workbook)
